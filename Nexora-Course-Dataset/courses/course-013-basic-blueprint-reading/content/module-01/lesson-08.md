The machined features in this section are common terms related to basic industry processes. These terms are often found on prints. For a better understanding of these processes, look at the models of machined features in the Print Reading Lab. **Bevel** A surface cut at an angle. In regard to welding, a bevel will normally end up being a surface prep for a weld.   ![Block with a 45 degree but for welding called a bevel.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image125.jpg) **Boss** A circular pad on forgings or castings, which project out from that body of the part. The surface of the boss is machined smooth for a bold head to seat on and it has a hole drilled through to accommodate the bolt shank. ![Piece of metal with a raised surface for welding called a boss.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image126.jpg) **Chamfer** A process of cutting away a sharp external corner or edge. Not for welding. ![Block cut at an angle to remove the sharp edge.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image127.jpg) **Counterbore** To enlarge drilled hole to a given diameter and depth. Usually done for recessing a bolt head. ![Block of material showing an example of a counterbore.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image128.jpg) **Countersink** To machine a conical depression in a drilled hole for recessing flathead screws or bolts. ![Part with a countersink hole to accommodate a tapered screw..](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image129.jpg) **Dovetail** A slot of any depth and width, which has angled sides. ![Image showing a dovetail cut in a piece of material.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image130.jpg)   Quiz Directions: Name the machined features shown below. ![graphic quiz for identification of surfaces and holes.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image131.jpg) **Fillet** A small radius filling formed between the inside angle of two surfaces. ![Material showing a fillet.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image132.jpg) **Kerf** The narrow slot formed by removing material while sawing or other machining. ![Material showing a kerf](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image133.jpg) **Keyway** A narrow groove or slot cut in the shaft hole of a sleeve or hub for accommodating a key. **Keyseat** A narrow groove or slot cut in a shaft for accommodating a key. ![Image showing a keyseat.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image134.jpg) **Knurl** To uniformly roughen with a diamond or straight pattern a cylindrical or flat surface.   ![Example of different knurl types.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image136.jpg) **Lug** A piece projecting out from the body of a part. Usually rectangular in cross section with a hole or slot in it. ![Showing a lug welded onto a piece of material for purpose of attachment.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image137.jpg) **Neck** To machine a narrow groove on a cylindrical part or object. ![Shows cuts made on a lathe into a piece of round stock.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image138.jpg) Quiz Directions: Name the machined features shown below. Check your answer. ![Visual quiz to identify the surfaces discussed.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image139.jpg) **Additional features and shapes.** **Pad** A slightly raised surface projecting out from the body of a part. The pad surface can be of any size or shape. (Remember, bosses can only be round) ![Shows a pad welded onto a part.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image140.jpg) **Round** A small radius rounded outside corner formed between two surfaces. ![Show the rounding off of a sharp edge.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image141.jpg) **Spline** A gear-like serrated surface on a shaft. Take the place of a key when more torque strength is required. ![A gear shape.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image142.jpg) **Spotface** A round surface on a casting or forging for a bold head. Usually about 1/16” deep. ![A round surface on a casting or forging for a bold head.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image143.jpg) **T-Slot** A slot of any dimensions to resemble a “T”. ![A slot of any dimensions to resemble a “T”.](https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/37/2019/06/image144.jpg) Quiz Directions: Name that machined features shown below.
