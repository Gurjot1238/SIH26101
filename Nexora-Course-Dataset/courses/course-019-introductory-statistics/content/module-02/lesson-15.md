Jesse graduated 37th out of a class of 180 students. There are 180 – 37 = 143 students ranked below Jesse. There is one rank of 37.

_x_ = 143 and _y_ = 1.  x+0.5y n x+0.5y n (100) =  143+0.5(1) 180 143+0.5(1) 180 (100) = 79.72. Jesse’s rank of 37 puts him at the 80th percentile.
