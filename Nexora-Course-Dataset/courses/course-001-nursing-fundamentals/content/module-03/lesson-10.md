## Learning Activities

(Answers to "Learning Activities" can be found in the "Answer Key" at the end of the book. Answers to interactive activity elements will be provided within the element as immediate feedback.)

  1. Test yourself for implicit bias at the [Learning for Justice website.](<https://www.learningforjustice.org/professional-development/test-yourself-for-hidden-bias>)
  2. Consider the following scenario.

You are completing the admission assessment for Mr. Xiong, a 64-year-old client admitted to the medical-surgical floor with acute kidney injury. Mr. Xiong speaks Hmong and some English. What actions should be undertaken to ensure that you are providing culturally responsive care to Mr. Xiong?
